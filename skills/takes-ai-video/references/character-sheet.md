# Character sheet prompt

Fill every bracket. A bracket left in the prompt is drawn as text. Call
`make_image(format="16:9", name="sheet-<name>")`. Later stills and clips get this file as a
reference, with "the person from the character sheet".

```
A true-to-life photographic character sheet of ONE made-up [woman / man / person] in their
[age range], [height and build]. [Skin tone] skin with real, unretouched texture: visible pores,
fine hair, slightly uneven tone, small marks. [Face shape], [cheekbones], [nose], [lips],
[brows], and a face that is not quite symmetrical. Expression: [expression], looking into the
camera, [attitude].

The feature people remember: [usually the hair: length, colour, texture, how it is worn].
They wear [accessory 1, with material], [accessory 2].

Clothes: [top: fit, colour, fabric, neckline, sleeves]. [Bottoms: cut, colour, fabric, how they
fall at the hem]. [Shoes: style, colour, material], no visible brand.

They look [3 or 4 words], and like a real person.

LAYOUT: one 16:9 image with TWO views of exactly the same person.
LEFT: a large front close-up of the whole face, with the skin texture, [the feature], [each
accessory seen at this size] and the top's neckline.
RIGHT: the whole body from head to feet, standing straight, arms loose at the sides, feet in the
frame, with the same face, [the feature], [every accessory], [top], [bottoms] and [shoes].

Both views on a pure white (#FFFFFF) seamless studio background. Documentary studio photograph,
full-frame camera, 85 mm lens, soft daylight softbox, gentle shadows, true colour. No retouching,
no airbrushing, no beauty filter, no skin smoothing. Real fabric detail on [the main fabrics],
natural creases and a little wear, real hands, adult proportions.

Avoid: [what they do NOT wear: bag, hat, rings, watch], text, logos, other people, watermarks,
a CGI or drawn look.
```

## Check the sheet

- Both views show the same face, hair, clothes and accessories.
- The skin has texture. A smooth, perfect face: add "ordinary person, not a model" and make it again.
- Feet are in the frame, and each hand has five fingers.
- No letters anywhere.
