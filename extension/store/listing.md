# Edge Add-ons listing

**Name:** Record in Takes

**Category:** Productivity

**Short description:** Select words on any page and record them as a video script in Takes, the free Mac teleprompter.

**Description:**

Found the words for your next video in a blog post, a doc or your notes? Select them and send them to Takes.

Takes is a free, open-source Mac app for talking-head videos: write the script, read it off the teleprompter, record, and get the edit and the posts.

How it works:
- Select words on a page, then right-click › Record in Takes, or click the Takes button.
- Change the title or the words if you like.
- Takes opens them as a new session with the words as the script, ready to record.

Private by design: the extension sends nothing to any server. It hands the words straight to the Takes app on your Mac.

Needs Takes for Mac (free): https://gettakes.app

**Website:** https://gettakes.app
**Support:** https://github.com/marvtub/takes-public/issues
**Privacy policy:** The extension collects no data. Selected words go only to the Takes app on the same Mac, through a takes:// link.
